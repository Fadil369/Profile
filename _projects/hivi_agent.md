## Overview

**HIVI** (Health Integrated Virtual Intelligence) is the front-facing agent for the healthcare pillar of the BrainSAIT ecosystem — one chat surface that adapts its persona to whoever is on the other end of the conversation, while every turn is routed through [MasterLINC](https://github.com/Fadil369/masterlinc) for orchestration.

## Personas

HIVI ships with six built-in personas, switchable mid-conversation:

- **Nour** — patient companion (default persona)
- **Leila** — family health assistant
- **Kesha** — caregiver support
- **Omar** — insurer / payer liaison
- **Atlas** — audit & compliance reviewer
- **Aiden** — NPHIES-specific assistant
