## Overview

**Sibaiti General Trading Co. Ltd** (شركة سبيتي للتجارة العامة المحدودة) is a private Sudanese company, headquartered in Khartoum, founded in 2015 under Sudan's Companies Act as a limited liability company. It operates across general trade, vegetable oils, grains, and agricultural project development.

## Business lines

- **Oilseeds & grains** — import, export, and investment in oilseeds and grains
- **Vegetable oils** — sourcing and refining to strict quality standards
- **Agricultural projects** — end-to-end development from source to market
- **General trade** — a broad trading network serving local and international clients
- **Logistics & supply** — reliable supply-chain management from shipment to delivery

## Why it's positioned under BIKI

Sibaiti represents a deliberate diversification of the founder's portfolio beyond healthcare — commodity trading rooted in Sudan, run with the same transparency and long-term partnership principles applied across the BrainSAIT ecosystem.
