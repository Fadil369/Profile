## Overview

**BrainSAIT Spark** turns a healthcare idea into a funded startup across six AI-powered phases — brainstorm, story, brand, PRD, code, GitHub — purpose-built for Vision 2030 founders.

The **BrainSAIT Incubator** extends that into an ongoing home for founders: an app store, partner network, clinics hub, innovation toolkit, courses, and templates for anyone building a Saudi healthcare startup.

## Companion programs

- **BrainSAIT Academy** — trains providers on NPHIES, RCM, and the full platform toolset
- **GTM Playbook** — go-to-market guidance for healthcare AI founders
- **Training courses** — including *Collective Brainpower* and *NPHIES-AI Mastery*, also mirrored on Google Classroom
