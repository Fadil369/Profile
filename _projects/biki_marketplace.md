## Overview

**BIKI** (Business Integrated Knowledge Intelligence) fronts the commercial side of the BrainSAIT ecosystem. Its flagship live surface is the **BrainSAIT AI Marketplace** — a bilingual (Arabic-first) storefront for software, courses, books, and advisory services, priced in SAR with instant licensing.

## Catalog

At the last audit pass the marketplace listed **97 products** across nine categories: marketing, healthcare, business, development, operations, creative, MOM, AI/strategy, and software solutions — plus a forms category for intake and procurement.

## Commerce features

- Instant digital licensing on purchase
- SAR-native checkout
- Bilingual catalog and cart (Arabic/English)
- Guest checkout with order tracking
