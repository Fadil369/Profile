## Overview

**Rafa Polyclinic** (Riyadh Almonsiah, Exit 8 Dammam Road) runs on **Abeer**, a connected-care platform that unifies family medicine, specialty care, pharmacy, and optical services behind one booking, chat, and payments layer.

## AI Care Concierge — Danah

**Danah** is Rafa's AI booking agent, available on web, WhatsApp, and Telegram. She checks live specialist availability, books across facilities, sends reminders, and can take payment on the spot.

## Portals

- **Patient Portal** — appointments, records, prescriptions, billing
- **Doctor Portal** — schedule, patient queue, e-prescribing, clinical notes
- **Nursing Portal** — ward tasks, vitals capture, medication administration
- **RCM Portal** — claims, denials, payer mix, collections in real time

## Connected infrastructure

Abeer runs on shared BrainSAIT infrastructure: **Danah** (conversational booking), **Hermes** (cross-system orchestration), a **Telegram concierge**, **AmmerPay** for online payments, automated reminder/statement mail, and the **BrainSAIT Market** for pharmacy and optical e-commerce fulfillment.
