## Overview

**BrainSAIT Education** is a bilingual (Arabic/English) professional-development program built for clinicians, builders, and decision-makers, teaching how open medical datasets translate into enterprise-grade healthcare AI in the Saudi context.

## What's live (MVP v0.1)

- **CheXpert Authority Page** — bilingual explainer with an annotated diagram, myth-busting table, glossary, and self-check quiz
- **Lead Magnet Guide** — print-ready PDF with reflection boxes and one-click export
- **3-Lesson Mini-Course** — datasets → Saudi localisation → research-to-enterprise, each lesson closing with a scored quiz
- **9-Dataset Catalog** — live from the Cloudflare edge, spanning x-ray, MRI, CT, histology, EHR, and genomics data, each mapped to a real use case

## Design principles

Bilingual by default, PDPL-aligned, and built around retrieval practice rather than passive reading — every module ends with a graded check.
