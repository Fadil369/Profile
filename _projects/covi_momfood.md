## Overview

**COVI** (Community Virtual Intelligence) fronts everything built for people and community rather than transactions. Its flagship live surface is **MomFood** — a home-cooked meals marketplace connecting families with local home chefs, built for community food culture, nutrition, and convenience.

## Voice-first by design

MomFood runs on the same conversational AI stack as [Basma](https://hnh.brainsait.org) (BrainSAIT's bilingual voice concierge), through the **ZuZu** voice agent, extending the ecosystem's reach from clinics into everyday community life.

## Why it belongs to COVI

MomFood is a deliberate signal that the BrainSAIT stack isn't only for hospitals — the same orchestration, payments, and voice infrastructure that runs a polyclinic can just as easily run a neighborhood cloud kitchen.
