## Overview

Two API-first products give the business pillar a government-data and gov-tech edge:

### Wathq Developer Hub

Production-grade, pay-per-request access to Saudi Commercial Registry, National Address, Real Estate Deeds, Power of Attorney, and E-Delegation APIs, billed against a prepaid balance.

### TrustOS — Sovereign AI Platform

AI agents that analyze Saudi government tender kits, NPHIES medical claims, and government contracts, with a free-tier demo (three complimentary queries, no sign-up).

## Why they matter

Both products turn government and regulatory data into structured, queryable intelligence — a natural extension of the same FHIR/NPHIES rigor BrainSAIT applies in healthcare, now applied to commercial and government data.
