<div class="en-content">

I bridge **clinical medicine** and **artificial intelligence** — building systems that make healthcare smarter, faster, and more human across the Middle East and Africa.

Over **10 years** at the bedside — in ICUs, neurosurgery wards, and emergency rooms — gave me an insider's grasp of what healthcare truly needs. Today, as Founder & CEO of [BrainSAIT](https://brainsait.org), I channel that clarity into AI products deployed in hospitals from Riyadh to Khartoum.
